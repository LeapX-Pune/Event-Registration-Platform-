Help Center

Find answers to common questions about EventPulse.

How do I register for an event?

Browse events, click on one to view details, then click "Register Now". You will need to sign in first.

Can I cancel my registration?

Contact the event organizer directly. Our contact form is available on event detail pages.

How do I create an account?

Click "Sign In" in the navigation bar, then select "Sign Up" to create a new account.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes, all transactions are encrypted and processed securely.

How do I contact support?

Use the contact form on any page or email us directly.
