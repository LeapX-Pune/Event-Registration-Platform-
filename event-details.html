Starlight Symphony is a three-day immersive music festival celebrating the intersection of classical orchestration and cutting-edge electronic music. Headlined by world-class artists, the event features multiple stages, visual art installations, and curated experiences for true music enthusiasts.

From ambient soundscapes to high-energy beats — food vendors, merchandise stalls, and an immersive art zone surround the main stages. Gates open at 5:00 PM each evening.
