About This Event

Prepare to witness an extraordinary fusion of futuristic electronic rhythms and classical orchestration. The Starlight Symphony returns for its annual showcase, bringing together legendary synth pioneers and a live 60-piece symphonic orchestra to reimagine the music of the past, present, and future.

Experience immersive 3D spatial audio, high-resolution laser projections, and custom light installations tailored to sync with every beat and violin sweep. This year's lineup features award-winning global electronic music producers cooperating with master conductors to deliver a breathtaking three-day concert experience.

Whether you are an electronic music fan or a classical symphony enthusiast, this event promises to break boundaries and create unforgettable memories under the stars of New York's premier arena.
